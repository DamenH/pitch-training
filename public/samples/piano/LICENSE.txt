Source recordings: Yamaha C5 grand piano, https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
MP3 encodings as distributed with Tone.js (https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/).

Changes made for this app: only the samples C1–A7 (every minor third) are included, and each
file was shortened to ~4.6 s by cutting at an MP3 frame boundary (no re-encoding; see
scripts/trim-mp3.py). Playback applies per-sample tuning corrections so notes sound at exact
12-tone equal temperament (A4 = 440 Hz).
